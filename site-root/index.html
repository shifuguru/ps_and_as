P's & A's

A fast trick-taking card game where finishing order matters. Empty your hand first to become President. Finish last and you're the Asshole — and you'll pay for it next round.

About the game

P's & A's is a digital version of the classic climbing / shedding card game often called Presidents, Asshole, or Scum. Play a quick match against AI, or create an online room and invite friends with a room code. The web app works on phones, tablets, and desktop — no install required.

What makes a round interesting

  • Ranks carry over. President and Asshole trade cards before the next deal.
  • Power cards matter. 2s, Jokers, and four-of-a-kind can dominate a trick — but the trick only ends when everyone else passes.
  • Runs clear hands fast. Consecutive ranks played together are one of the strongest ways to shed cards.
  • The 10 Rule. Playing a 10 lets you choose whether the next play must go higher or lower.

How to play

Players take turns playing cards onto the table. Each play must beat what is currently showing — a higher single, pair, triple, four-of-a-kind, or run of the same length. If you cannot or do not want to beat it, press Pass. When every other active player has passed, the trick ends and the last player leads the next one.

Important: A powerful card does not automatically end the trick — not a 2, not a Joker, not four of a kind. The trick only ends when every other active player passes.

Special cards

2

The strongest normal card. It can only be beaten by a Joker or four 2s (if fewer than four are already on the table). Playing a 2 does not end the trick.

Joker

The strongest card in the game. It beats every normal play and stays on the table until every other player passes. Jokers cannot be played while a run is active.

Four of a kind

Four matching cards played together are one of the strongest plays. They can only be beaten by a higher four of a kind or a Joker. If four of a kind are built up across turns in the same trick, that play becomes unbeatable — everyone else must pass.

The 10 Rule

Playing a 10 lets you choose the direction of the next play: higher than 10 or lower than 10. The effect lasts for one turn. Runs ignore the 10 Rule.

Runs

A run is one play made of consecutive ranks, all with the same count — for example 4-5-6, 8-9-10-J, or pair runs like 7-7 / 8-8 / 9-9. A valid run must include at least three ranks, be consecutive (A-2-3 is illegal), and use the same number of cards at each rank.

To beat a run, play a higher run of the same length and count, or extend it with the next adjacent rank while the run is still active.

Starting a round

Every round opens with 3♣. Whoever holds it leads. In two-player games, a hidden Dead Hand balances the deck; if it contains 3♣, play opens with 3♠ instead.

Finishing order & ranks

Where you finish sets your title for the next round:

Finish Rank
First President
Second (5+ players) Vice President
Middle Citizen
Second-to-last (5+ players) Vice Asshole
Last Asshole

From round two onward, the Asshole gives their best card(s) to the President, who sends weaker cards back. With five or more players, Vice President and Vice Asshole also exchange one card each.

Game modes

Quick Game

Jump into a match against AI opponents. Good for learning the rules or a short session. Works offline once the page has loaded.

Online multiplayer

Create a room, share the code with friends, and play for the Presidency. If your connection drops briefly, you can reconnect and rejoin the table.

Beginner tips

  • Don't waste your 2s too early — they are your best normal cards.
  • Jokers are often worth saving for a decisive trick.
  • Passing can be the strongest move when you cannot beat the pile cleanly.
  • Runs clear a lot of cards quickly when the table is open.
  • Think about the next trick, not just the card you are playing now.

Frequently asked questions

Can I play offline?
Yes. Quick Game works without an internet connection after the app has loaded once.
Can I play with friends?
Yes. Create an online room in the game and share the room code.
Does suit matter?
Normally no. Suits only decide who opens with 3♣ (or 3♠ in two-player).
Why can't I beat a Joker?
Nothing normally beats a Joker, and you cannot play a Joker into an active run.
Is the game free?
Yes. The browser version is free to play. Optional in-app purchases may be offered to support hosting costs.
