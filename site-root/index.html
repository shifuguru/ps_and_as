01 / The premise

One hand. One table.

Presidents & Assholes is a shedding game with a social twist. Play sets of cards to beat the table, pass when you can't play, but race to finish your hand or end up as the Asshole. Your place becomes your title for the next round. Every deal gives you a new advantage to protect, or a new hell to escape.

Read the Table

Watch carefully, decide when to play your strongest cards, or pass to leave yourself a way out for the end-game.

Become President

Finish first to become President, and gain an advantage in the next round by trading your worst card, for the Asshole's best.

Play Your Way

Read the table. Play aggressively, or hold onto your strongest cards by passing, waiting for the right moment...

02 / At the table

Simple to start.

It's the same question: can you beat the current pile, and is it worth doing right now?

1

Lead the Round

3♣ begins the game.


Play a single card, pairs, triples, or four-of-a-kind - if you've got them.

2

Play or Pass

Play ascends from the pile's current set of cards, matching the count. Pass, however, and you must wait for the pile to be cleared.

3

Finish First

Empty your hand before everyone else to become President for the next round. The rest of the table continues until every position is decided.

05 / Rules desk

Know the ladder before you sit down.

Cards ascend beginning from 3, through King, Ace, 2, and finally Joker. Play the same number of cards to beat a set. Runs need at least three consecutive ranks with the same count. A trick clears when every other active player passes.

The useful surprise: a 2, Joker, or four-of-a-kind does not automatically end the trick. Power is only useful if you can control what happens after it.

06 / First strategy notes

Small decisions that change a hand.

These are the fundamentals to carry into your first table. The full rules page covers the edge cases.

01

Save your ceiling.
A 2 or Joker can win a key exchange, but only if you still have a plan for the cards left behind.

02

Passing is information.
A pass protects your future turn and tells the table that this rank may be safe to lead later.

03

Runs are an exit.
When the table is open, consecutive cards can empty your hand faster than a string of singles.

04

Play for the next round.
Finishing order determines the trade, so a narrow win can be better than a flashy trick.

07 / Questions before the deal

Good things to know.

How many players can play?
The game supports multiplayer tables, with a two-player dead hand used to balance a two-player game. Five or more players add Vice President and Vice Asshole trades.
Can I play offline?
Yes. The local quick-game path uses CPU opponents and does not need an online room after the app has loaded.
Can I play on mobile?
Yes. The browser game is responsive and uses tap controls on mobile, with an installable web-app flow available in supported browsers.
Can I play online?
Yes. Create or join an online room, then share the room code with friends.
Is the game free?
Yes. The browser game is free to play.
Where do I begin?
Open the game, choose a local quick game or online play, and follow the in-game setup.
