## Multiplayer

- **Opening lead after trades** — Online, whoever holds **3♣** starts the round after President/Asshole trades finish, even when the Asshole received a different rank-3 card (e.g. 3♥). All players should see the same opener after sync.
- **Round transitions** — Post-trade turn ownership is recalculated on the server before the next sync, so a stale “dealer’s left” or wrong-seat opener is less likely to flash on clients.
- **Find Game** — When no human public lobbies are open, the list shows **“No Public Games Available”** instead of a bot table. Joining by code **BOTOPN** is blocked with a clear message (public bot matchmaking deferred).

---

## Gameplay

- **On Top! (10s)** — Winning a trick with a higher or lower **10** should grant **On Top!** again after reconnect-style sync stripped the direction choice; you can play or pass on your on-top turn.
- **On Top! (runs)** — Run winners still receive **On Top!** when everyone else passes; the table should not finalize the trick before the on-top player acts.
- **Dead Hand** — When the dead hand holds **3♣**, living players still follow the correct alternate opening rules after trades (unchanged rules; protected by regression tests).

---

## Stability

- **Server turn checks** — The server accepts valid actions from the on-top winner instead of returning “Not your turn” when sync briefly pointed at another seat.
- **Client opener reconcile** — If a late `gameStateSync` arrives after trades complete but before anyone plays, the client corrects the opener to the **3♣** holder when authoritative hands are present.

---

## UI / UX

- **Find Game empty state** — Copy updated to **“No Public Games Available”** with guidance to host or try again later.
