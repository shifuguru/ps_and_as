Presidents & Assholes

Privacy policy for the Ps & As web and mobile game.

Last updated: 4 August 2026

What this game is

Presidents & Assholes (“Ps & As”) is a multiplayer card game. You can play offline against AI or online in shared rooms. The live web game is hosted on GitHub Pages; the multiplayer server runs on Railway.

Information we use

  • Display name — chosen by you (up to 20 characters). Other players in your room can see it.
  • Player identifiers — a device/install ID, and when you sign in, an Apple Game Center player ID and/or a Google account id (`google:…`).
  • Game Center — on iOS we may use Game Center to identify you in multiplayer. Your Game Center display name and player ID can be shared with other players in your game.
  • Google Sign-In (optional, web) — if you link Google, we verify a Google ID token and may store a short-lived server session so your XP and profile can sync across devices. Google may provide a subject id and, depending on consent, an email or name used only for account linking — not shown to other players as your table name unless you choose that display name.
  • Progress & preferences — XP, career stats, appearance, and table colour prefs. Stored on your device and, when synced, on our game server.
  • Live game state — room membership and play actions while you are in a match. Hands are not broadcast to opponents.

We do not process payment cards in the app. Optional donations use GitHub Sponsors.

Where data lives

  • On your device or browser (local storage).
  • On our Railway game server for online rooms and optional cloud stats.
  • With Apple or Google when you use their sign-in products (their policies also apply).

How we use it

  • Run multiplayer games and reconnect you to your seat when possible.
  • Keep XP / profile in sync when you link an account.
  • Show your display name and public progress to people at your table.
  • Keep the service secure (for example, verifying Google tokens before writing cloud stats).

We do not sell personal information. We do not run third-party advertising SDKs in this game.

Retention

  • Local data stays until you clear site/app storage or uninstall.
  • Cloud stats stay until deleted or the server store is reset.
  • Google sync sessions expire after about 30 days unless renewed.
  • Live room data ends when the room ends.

Your choices

  • Play without Google Sign-In (local / device identity).
  • Change your display name in Settings.
  • Clear browser or app storage to remove local data.
  • Request deletion of a cloud stats record by contacting us (see below) with your player ID from Settings / sync status when available.

Children

The game is a general-audience card game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for marketing. If you believe a child’s data should be removed, contact us.

Contact

Privacy and data deletion: security policy on GitHub, or open a private security advisory. For non-sensitive questions you can also use the repository’s GitHub contact options for @shifuguru.

Maintainer technical inventory (for operators): see docs/data-inventory.md in the source repository.
