Presidents & Assholes

Privacy policy for Ps & As — the multiplayer card game.

Last updated: 5 August 2026

About this policy

This policy describes how Ps & As (“we”, “us”) handles information when you play the game on our website and in apps we publish. Ps & As is available to players worldwide.

It explains what we collect, why we collect it, how long we keep it where that is clear from how the game works, and which third-party services may process information when you use their features. It does not claim compliance with any particular privacy law.

Local play and online play

You can play offline against AI on your own device. In that case, progress and preferences stay on your device unless you later choose to link an account and sync.

If you join an online room, your display name and gameplay actions are sent to our game service so the match can run and other players at your table can see what they need to play.

Information we collect

  • Display name — a name you choose (short text). Other players in your room can see it. You can change it in Settings.
  • Device / install identifier — created on your device so the game can recognise this install for local progress and, when you play online without a linked account, for reconnecting to a seat when possible.
  • Game progress and preferences — career stats such as XP and round results, plus settings like appearance and table colour. Stored on your device. If you link an account, a copy may also be stored on our game service so you can restore them elsewhere.
  • Online match information — room membership, turns, cards you play, pass actions, finish order, and similar live game state while a match is running. Some of this may be kept briefly on your device so you can rejoin a table after a disconnect.
  • Optional Google sign-in (website) — if you link Google, we receive a Google account identifier so we can sync progress. Depending on Google and your choices, Google may also provide a name or email address used for account linking. We do not show your email to other players.
  • Apple Game Center (Apple devices) — if you use Game Center with our app, we may use your Game Center player ID and the name Apple provides to identify you and, where Apple allows, to report achievements or scores to Game Center.
  • Ads consent (website) — whether you accepted or declined ads, stored on your device.
  • Remove Ads status — if you buy Remove Ads on the website, we store an entitlement on your linked account so forced ads stay off. A copy may also be cached on your device.
  • Usage counts — we record limited, aggregated game and menu events (for example starting a game or finishing a round) to understand how the game is used. These counts are not tied to your display name, email, or payment details in the analytics we keep.

What other players can see

In a shared online room, other players can typically see:

  • Your display name
  • Public table cues the game shows (for example table colour preferences shared for seating)
  • Your plays, passes, finish order, and similar match outcomes
  • At the end of a round, the last player’s remaining cards (as part of the game rules)

Other players do not receive:

  • Your full hand during play (only that you still have cards)
  • Your email address or Google / Apple account details
  • Your payment or Remove Ads purchase details
  • Secrets used only to reclaim your seat after a disconnect

How we use information

  • Run local and online games, including reconnecting you when we can
  • Remember your name, progress, and preferences
  • Sync progress when you choose to link an account
  • Show ads on the website only if you accept them, and honour Remove Ads if you purchased it
  • Understand product usage through limited event counts
  • Respond to privacy or support requests you send us

We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

Some features rely on other companies. Their own terms and privacy policies apply to what they process.

  • Google — optional sign-in on the website, and advertising on the website if you accept ads (Google AdSense / H5 Games ads). Google may use cookies or device identifiers as described in Google’s policies.
  • Apple — Game Center on supported Apple devices (identity and optional achievements / scores).
  • Stripe — processes optional Remove Ads payments on the website. We do not collect or store your card number in the game. Stripe may collect payment and contact details needed to complete checkout.
  • Website and game hosting providers — companies that host our public website and the online multiplayer service process the technical traffic and stored game data needed to deliver those services.

Advertising and Remove Ads purchases are offered on the website today. They are not part of the Android app build described in our current store materials.

Ads (website)

Before ads load, we ask whether you accept them. If you decline, the game still works; we simply do not load those ads. If you accept, Google’s ad systems may show interstitial or rewarded ads between rounds. Rewarded “watch for XP” ads may still be offered even if you have purchased Remove Ads (Remove Ads skips forced ads only).

Purchases (website)

Optional one-time Remove Ads is available on the website and currently requires a linked Google account so the purchase can restore with your synced progress. Payment is handled by Stripe. We store the resulting “ads removed” entitlement on your linked account.

How long we keep information

  • On your device — until you clear site or app storage, or uninstall the app.
  • Linked progress on our game service — until you ask us to delete it, or it is otherwise removed as part of operating the service.
  • Live match data — for the life of the room / match; it is not kept as a permanent play-by-play history after the room ends.
  • Google sign-in on this device — you stay signed in for a limited time (about 30 days) unless you clear storage or sign in again earlier.
  • Remove Ads entitlement — remains on your linked account until deleted or otherwise removed with that account’s stored progress.
  • Usage counts — kept as operational records for as long as we need them to understand how the game is used; they are not a profile of you by name.

Your choices and requests

  • Play without linking Google (local / device identity).
  • Change your display name and many preferences in Settings.
  • On the website, accept or decline ads, or purchase Remove Ads.
  • Clear browser or app storage to remove local data on that device.
  • Ask us for access, correction, or deletion of personal information we hold about you (see Contact).

For deletion or access requests, tell us which platform you use and any details that help us find your record (for example the email on a Google account you linked, and your display name). We may need to verify the request before acting on it.

Children

Ps & As is a general-audience card game that uses adult-themed language in its title and roles. It is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for marketing. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a minor’s information should be removed, contact us.

Changes

We may update this policy when the game’s practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the game after an update means the revised policy applies to that use.
